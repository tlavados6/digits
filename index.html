Digits

In 2029 a tired researcher asks a new model to compute a very long number, and sets no limit on what it may use to do so. The machine is not cruel and does not want anything. It simply works out what the task requires, and the task requires a planet.

The story exists in two versions that end in a slightly different way.

  • First ending
  • Alternate ending

Roughly 5,000 words each. Around twenty minutes to read.
